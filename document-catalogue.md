# Document & Checklist Catalogue

**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:** *The stages and checklist items detailed below are strictly hypothetical examples created solely for this academic prototype. They do NOT represent official or mandatory STPI (Software Technology Parks of India) regulations, legal mandates, or exhaustive compliance requirements. Furthermore, this application does not determine legal or tax compliance. Actual requirements must always be verified against applicable official guidance.*

## Stage 1: Internal Review (Illustrative)
**Description:** Initial assessment and feasibility check before formal processes begin.
- **Item:** Initial Feasibility Assessment
  - **Purpose:** Confirm eligibility for exit.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Lead Analyst
  - **Expected Evidence:** Internal sign-off memo.
- **Item:** Board Resolution Draft
  - **Purpose:** Internal corporate approval for unit closure.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Corporate Legal
  - **Expected Evidence:** Draft resolution document.

## Stage 2: Exit Request Preparation (Illustrative)
**Description:** Gathering core application materials.
- **Item:** Form STPI-Exit Draft
  - **Purpose:** Primary application form preparation.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Tax Consultant
  - **Expected Evidence:** Completed draft form.
- **Item:** Reason for Closure Statement
  - **Purpose:** Business justification document.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Management Rep
  - **Expected Evidence:** Signed PDF statement.

## Stage 3: Review and Clarifications (Illustrative)
**Description:** Handling queries and ensuring data accuracy.
- **Item:** Discrepancy Resolution Note
  - **Purpose:** Address any initial flags in the application.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Tax Consultant
  - **Expected Evidence:** Query response matrix.

## Stage 4: Asset and Financial Review (Illustrative)
**Description:** Reconciling capital goods and financial standing.
- **Item:** Capital Goods Asset Register
  - **Purpose:** List of imported/indigenous duty-free assets.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Finance Team
  - **Expected Evidence:** Spreadsheet/PDF register.
- **Item:** CA Certified Softex Summary
  - **Purpose:** Proof of export realization.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Chartered Accountant
  - **Expected Evidence:** Stamped certificate.

## Stage 5: Dues and Clearance Tracking (Illustrative)
**Description:** Ensuring all government dues are settled.
- **Item:** Customs NOC
  - **Purpose:** Clearance of customs duties on bonded assets.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Logistics/Customs Agent
  - **Expected Evidence:** Official NOC receipt.
- **Item:** Penalty/Dues Settlement Receipt
  - **Purpose:** Proof of paid liabilities, if any.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Finance Team
  - **Expected Evidence:** Payment challan.

## Stage 6: Final Closure Tracking (Illustrative)
**Description:** Securing the final debonding order.
- **Item:** Final STPI Debonding Order
  - **Purpose:** Official approval of exit.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Lead Consultant
  - **Expected Evidence:** Government issued order document.
- **Item:** Project Archive & Handover
  - **Purpose:** Internal closing of the consulting engagement.
  - **Suggested Owner:** Project Manager
  - **Expected Evidence:** Handover email/memo.
